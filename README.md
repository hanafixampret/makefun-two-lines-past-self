Entry for the [Two Lines to Your Past Self](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suga-two-lines-to-your-past-self-f36c83ca) bounty on make.fun.

The program writes two lines to `/output/entry.txt`: a warning to my past self about one specific moment — telling the team "we don't need a backup" in the Friday standup before the migration — and one sentence on why it mattered: the migration wiped the only copy, and the weekend went to rebuilding three years of work from memory.

Run with `node main.ts` (Node 22+).
